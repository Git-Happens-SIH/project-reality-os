How do I invite my site engineers and planners? Getting started

Open Settings → Team, then send email invites with a role: Site Engineer or Planner. Invites expire after 7 days. Engineers submit daily reports; planners review matches and approve schedule updates.

What should a daily site report include? Site reports

Note work completed, location or zone, crew count, blockers, and any delay risk. Photos and voice notes are optional but improve match confidence. Submit before end of shift so planners see same-day progress.

How does AI task matching work? Matching

After a report lands, the model proposes links to schedule tasks with a confidence score: Confirmed, Review Needed, or Possible Risk. Low-confidence matches never write to the live plan until a planner decides.

Who can approve a schedule update? Approvals

Only users with the Planner role. Site engineers submit and can re-edit drafts, but nothing hits the live schedule until a planner approves or rejects the proposed match. Human sign-off is always required.

Can I submit reports offline? Sync & offline

Yes. Drafts queue on device when the site has no signal. When connectivity returns, reports sync automatically and enter the matching queue. A pale-yellow banner marks items still waiting to upload.

What happens if a match looks wrong? Matching

Reject the suggestion and pick the correct task from the schedule, or leave it as Review Needed. Rejected links stay in history for audit but never update programme progress.

How do I change my organisation name or logo? Account

Go to Settings → Organisation. Admins can edit the display name and upload a square logo (PNG or SVG, max 2 MB). Changes appear on reports and the planner dashboard within a few minutes.

Do I need to replace my existing schedule tool? Getting started

No. Project Reality OS sits beside your programme — it closes the gap between field reports and what the plan believes. Connect your schedule export or API, then keep planning where you already work.
